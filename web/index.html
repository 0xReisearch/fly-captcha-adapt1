WHAT YOU ARE WATCHING

The fly brought a learning layer.

Real fruit photos stimulate a fixed, full-connectome spiking model based on the MaleCNS fly wiring map. Adapt-1 receives 64 pooled neural responses and learns whether to select or leave a tile.

The game returns correct/incorrect feedback on training tiles only. The final test uses different photos, with no feedback. The pictured walking is an animation of those decisions, not a simulation of biological locomotion.

The recording uses cached neural responses with Adapt-1 selecting tiles. In live mode, fly-side readouts select tiles and Adapt-1 manages additional inspections. Start a fresh learner with your own Neuroadapt key.

Photo split: unseen images, not a claim of unseen physical fruit specimens. This is an original parody game, not a test against commercial CAPTCHA security.

Full method and attribution

A fresh start.

Your key is sent over HTTPS to this demo server and held in memory during the run. It is not saved or logged. Learning requests use your Neuroadapt quota.
