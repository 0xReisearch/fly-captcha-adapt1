# I am not a robot. I am a fly.

A simulated fruit fly tries to pass a banana CAPTCHA, gets paid in imaginary
bananas, and then challenges you to take the same test.

**[Watch the recording](https://fly.reilabs.org/)** without a key, or
**[start your own live fly](https://fly.reilabs.org/live)** with your
Neuroadapt API key. This is a parody experiment, not a commercial CAPTCHA bypass.

Live mode runs the neural simulation again for every view. It does not load
cached sensory responses or a trained task checkpoint. The recording is a
separate reference experiment with a different readout architecture, below.

## Who does what?

| Component | Responsibility |
|---|---|
| Fixed MaleCNS connectome simulation | Converts the current retinal image into simulated neural activity |
| Artificial reward-plastic readouts | Choose select/leave and the direction of an additional inspection |
| Adapt-1 | Learns when to answer immediately and when to spend another visual inspection |
| Experiment environment | Presents photographs; scores choices after they are made; returns training reward |
| Original Fruitless anatomical assets and renderer | Show the fly on the CAPTCHA floor and measured neural activity |

**The fly-side readout chooses the tiles. Adapt-1 does not.** Adapt-1 receives
three measurements: decision margin, variation in the sensory response, and
experience count. It never receives a photo, fruit label, tile ID, or the fly's
full neural feature vector. Its actions are `answer_now` and `inspect_again`.

The fly simulation runs on CPU; the browser needs WebGL.

## What learns?

The simulation uses the fixed **MaleCNS v1** graph: 166,700 neurons, 25,582,938
directed neuron-pair edges, and 124,177,617 synaptic contacts. This is a simplified
leaky-integrate-and-fire model, not a claim to reproduce a living fly's cognition.

A photograph becomes retinal contrast and is presented for 60 simulated
milliseconds. Sixty-four optic-lobe pools summarize its spike counts. Fast neural
state carries from an overview into a closer inspection of that photo, then
resets before the next photo. Biological graph weights remain fixed.

Two artificial trainable readouts sit downstream of that activity. One chooses
select/leave. The other chooses a left, center, or right glimpse when allowed.
They use a fixed random nonlinear expansion and recursive least squares on the
chosen action's reward. Attention retains eligibility until the answer's outcome
arrives and discounts older feedback as the answer readout changes. These are
experimental artificial attachments, not reconstructed biological learning rules.

Adapt-1 separately learns the usefulness of another look. Correctness rewards
its selected budget policy, with a small additional-inspection cost. If Core
abstains, the fly answers from its first view; no imaginary inspection policy or
feedback is inserted.

The default overview is deliberately coarse: 2 x 2 pixels, upsampled to the
retina. A permitted glimpse provides detail. This is the declared task condition,
not a claim of unrestricted visual intelligence.

## Play

1. Watch the recording without a key, or choose **Try the live neural learner**.
2. On the live page, press **Start fresh** and enter your own Neuroadapt key.
3. Watch the baseline, one training pass, and frozen exam. Runtime depends on API
   latency. **Stop learning** cancels later operations after the current call.
4. After evaluation, choose **Another CAPTCHA**. Select photos on the 3D floor
   and **Lock my picks**, or choose **Fly goes solo**.
5. Repeat practice without retraining. **Forget session** releases the local
   learner and key once the active run has stopped.

Perfect boards earn the illustrated banana salary. Reactions and identity stamps
are scripted comedy, not statements generated by the neural system. Body motion
is illustrative; no locomotion controller is being learned.

Practice resamples existing test photos. Both learners remain frozen, practice
outcomes never enter feedback, and the original evaluation remains unchanged.
Human selections reach only the comparison scorer. Practice is play, not more
independent benchmark evidence.

## Protocol and evidence

The fixed Fruits-360 split has 360 training photos and 180 test photos, balanced
between bananas and non-bananas (apples, lemons and oranges). The manifest gives
source URLs and image hashes. Exact train/test overlap is checked. These are
held-out photos from the same categories, not necessarily unseen physical fruit
specimens or new categories.

Each live run:

1. Creates a new randomly named, API-key-owned Domain with transfer disabled and
   confirms zero feedback-policy samples. It never clears unrelated user data.
2. Starts untrained fly-side readouts and measures 180 baseline choices without
   feedback or learned inspection control.
3. Makes one pass over 360 training photos. Answers precede label access and
   feedback. Only the chosen action receives a reward.
4. Checks policy feedback admission and waits for training to settle periodically
   and before evaluation.
5. Evaluates 180 held-out photos without feedback. Checks fly readout hashes and
   public Core sample count, learner version and model metadata across testing.

Hosted verification checks **public learning-state observables**, not private
Core weight bytes. Query/audit state can still be written by the API. A fresh
Domain is not a fresh hosted agent installation; use a dedicated test key when
you need separation from unrelated applications.

A complete non-abstaining run uses about 940 production calls including state
checks; practice also consumes query quota. Calls are ordered. Ambiguous write
failures are not automatically retried. Incomplete runs receive no final score.

### Adapt-1's contribution to learning

With every system trained from scratch, **the fly with Adapt-1 reached 98.15%
held-out accuracy**, ahead of all tested fly-only acquisition controls on the
current coarse-view task.

| System | Mean held-out accuracy |
|---|---:|
| **Fly + Adapt-1** | **98.15%** |
| Fly alone, always inspect with learned gaze | 90.74% |
| Fly alone, fixed-crop inspection | 95.93% |
| Fly alone, random inspection timing with matched budgets | 96.48% |

Three initialization/order seeds; 360 training photos and 180 frozen held-out
photos per run, reusing the same split. Every system in the table receives 180
test inspections. The matched-random control also preserves Adapt-1's exact
training inspection count: **the +1.67-point gain comes with the same inspection
budget**. The gain over always inspecting with learned gaze is **7.41 points**.

See the [per-seed comparison and method](research/INSPECTION_COMPARISON.md)
and [complete comparison data](data/inspection-controller-comparison.json).

All 360 controller feedbacks were admitted. There were zero test feedbacks;

## Reproduce through production

Linux, Python 3.11+, a C++17 compiler, sufficient CPU/RAM, and a Neuroadapt key are
required. The controller connects to the Neuroadapt API.

```bash
python3 -m venv .venv
.venv/bin/pip install -r requirements.txt -r requirements-sensory.txt
.venv/bin/python setup_fly.py
OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 .venv/bin/python research/check_production.py \
  --vendor vendor/doomfly --seed 17 --output runs/my-live-experiment
```

The key prompt is hidden. Never put a key in a command, URL, committed file or
screenshot. Use a new output directory. `--limit 24` is a short contract check,
not the full protocol. The simulator is built from pinned upstream code and data.

Outputs: `domain.json`, `configuration.json`, `trace.jsonl`,
`core-api-trace.jsonl`, and completed `results.json`. Practice is written to
`practice.jsonl`. API traces contain bodies, not authorization headers. Treat
your exports as private application data.

For the website locally:

```bash
PUBLIC_ORIGIN=http://127.0.0.1:8795 OMP_NUM_THREADS=1 OPENBLAS_NUM_THREADS=1 \
  .venv/bin/uvicorn live_app:app --host 127.0.0.1 --port 8795 --workers 1 --no-access-log
```

Open `/` for the recording or `/live` for your production learner. For local Core,
see [research/README.md](research/README.md). Public deployment instructions are
in [DEPLOYMENT.md](DEPLOYMENT.md).

### Production routes

Base: `https://rei-neuroadapt-api.reilabs.org/api/v1`.

| Route | Use |
|---|---|
| `POST /domains` | Register a fresh inspection-budget Domain |
| `POST /domains/{id}/query` | Choose answer-now versus inspect-again |

## Privacy and limits

- Visitors supply their own key. There is no default, shared or fallback key.
- Keys travel over HTTPS and remain in server memory through practice. They are
  not stored in the browser, files, traces or request logs. The operator handles
  the key; only use trusted hosts. This is not forensic memory-erasure assurance.
- HttpOnly/SameSite cookies isolate state, controls and exports per browser.
  Same-origin POST checks reject cross-site use. No arbitrary upstream proxy exists.
- Two active CPU slots, three retained sessions, a one-minute start cooldown and
  a two-hour absolute lifetime bound the demo. Expiry/forgetting/restart release
  local state; your hosted Domain stays in your Neuroadapt account.
- Artifacts live temporarily in the private runtime directory. Export before
  expiry. They are removed on disposal and lost on service restart.
- Stop prevents later calls after the in-flight operation. Admitted remote
  feedback/training is not rolled back. Closing a tab alone does not stop it.
- No analytics, local-machine tunnel, commercial CAPTCHA interaction or GPU dependency.


The tests cover the production contract, visitor isolation, key handling, cleanup,
feedback and frozen inference. `research/check_browser.py` exercises the local
Core lab on an isolated server, including real neural simulation, 3D tile clicks,
repeat rounds, stopping and desktop/mobile rendering. Its local prerequisites
are in the research guide. Production checks consume your API quota.

The anatomical body/rig and connectome renderer are the specific assets from
[Fruitless](https://github.com/nicodunks/fruitless), revision
`0943b2c00b47a97c8e22438b11192b33d6779f56`. Highlights use measured simulation
counts joined to anatomical coordinates, not courtship recordings or random
decorative firing. Missing display coordinates do not remove simulated neurons.

- MaleCNS: HHMI Janelia and collaborators, [CC BY 4.0](https://male-cns.janelia.org/download/).
- Simulator: [DOOMFLY](https://github.com/nftechie/doomfly), MIT, revision
  `71ecf53d78eaffaf1a57ed7b0ccf5d458abc9f33`.
- Anatomical assets: NeuroMechFly / NeLy-EPFL, Apache-2.0. Third-party notices
  are retained under `licenses/`.
- Photos: provenance in `data/tiles.json`. Three.js and Lucide notices are bundled.

`live_app.py` is the visitor-isolated host; `research/` implements live perception,
